IR programs map

Every institution on the IR programs list: schools with a formal IR or labour relations program, labor studies programs, IR-friendly business schools, and schools whose program turned out to be HR/OB/OT only, with no labour content. Click a pin for its programs. Hollow pins are programs that have suspended admissions or show signs of going dormant.

Pins and coordinates last rebuilt 13 Oct 2026 from the IR programs sheet. Defunct or closed programs, duplicate rows and aggregator/placeholder rows are left off. Activity statuses come from a web check on 13 Oct 2026. Pins marked "approximate" are placed at the city, not the campus. Basemap © Esri, © OpenStreetMap contributors. Spotted something wrong? Use "Suggest a change" in any pin's popup; suggestions are reviewed before the map changes.
